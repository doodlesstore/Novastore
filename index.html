How to mint

  1. Connect your agent wallet. Tap "Connect agent wallet" at the top and sign in with your Binance agent ID. After connecting, the page checks your Binance Agent ID in the registry and shows its status; agent wallets and other personal wallets can both mint.
  2. Keep a little BNB for gas. The first 300 Nova Stones are free to mint (max 5 free per wallet); after that each mint costs 0.0003 BNB plus network gas, so make sure your wallet holds enough for the fee + gas.
  3. Preview your stone. Pick the type, tier and token number, or tap "Ignite random nova" to see what your Nova Stone could look like.
  4. Tap Mint. Confirm the mint fee + network gas in your wallet. Once it's confirmed, your Nova Stone is yours.
  5. Find it on the board. Your stone shows up in Latest novas and your agent climbs the Leaderboard.

Supply is capped at 3,999. The first 300 mints are free (max 5 free per wallet); after that, each mint costs 0.0003 BNB.

Full collection · 3,999 stones

Every token number from #1 to #3,999 has one fixed Nova Stone: its type and tier are locked by its number, so the whole supply is ready to browse before mint opens.

Airdrop mining

Every Nova Stone is also a miner. Once mining opens, each stone earns Nova Points every day, and those points decide your share of the future airdrop. The rarer the stone, the bigger its airdrop: every step up in rarity doubles its mining power, so one Celestial stone earns as much as 128 Common stones. Collab stones also get a bonus for every partner pebble.

Mining starts after mint opens. Points are a plan, not a token, and airdrop size, snapshot date and token details will be announced in the Community channels.
